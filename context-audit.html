What this is, and what it isn't

This is a diagnostic, not a measurement. There is no research establishing how many relationships a mentor can hold or how many contexts a founder can carry — anyone claiming a precise threshold is inventing it. What the audit does is turn a few things you already know into a shape you can look at.

We deliberately avoided producing a number out of ten. A score would imply a precision we do not have, and it would invite you to optimise the score rather than notice the pattern. You get a band and a description; the useful part is usually the handover question at the end.

If you want the underlying idea, it is context reconstruction — the work of rebuilding where something stands before you can contribute to it — and the property that removes it, cognitive continuity.
