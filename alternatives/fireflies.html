Fireflies.ai is excellent at capturing every word from your meetings. But a transcript is just raw material. My-CoMind turns that material into structured, queryable memory — so you never rebuild context from scratch.

What Fireflies.ai does exceptionally well

Fireflies has built one of the best meeting transcription products on the market. Its strengths include:

  • Accurate transcription — AI-powered speech-to-text across multiple speakers and accents
  • Meeting search — keyword search across all your recorded conversations
  • CRM integrations — auto-log calls to Salesforce, HubSpot, and others
  • Topic tracking — surface mentions of keywords across meetings

If your primary need is a searchable archive of everything said in meetings, Fireflies delivers.

Where Fireflies falls short for continuity

A transcript tells you what was said. It doesn't tell you:

  • "What decisions were made in this meeting, and which ones are still in effect?"
  • "How did our position on pricing change between March and June?"
  • "What open questions from last quarter still need answers?"

Fireflies gives you a pile of searchable text. You still do the hard work of reading, synthesizing, connecting, and remembering.

The three gaps

1. No decision extraction — Fireflies transcribes decisions as text, but doesn't identify them as decisions, track their status, or flag when they're superseded.

2. No cross-meeting connection — Meeting #12 and Meeting #34 might be about the same topic, but Fireflies treats them as isolated documents. The connection is manual.

3. No evolution tracking — You can't see how a position shifted over three months without manually opening and comparing transcripts side by side.
