How It Works

Three steps to never lose your thinking again.

1

Capture

Use voice or text to record a decision, insight, or conversation as it happens. In a meeting, on a walk, between tasks — wherever your thinking is, My-CoMind is there.

2

Connect

My-CoMind automatically categorizes your input and connects it to related thoughts across your history. What starts as a note becomes part of a living reasoning chain.

3

Continue

When you return — tomorrow, next week, or months later — ask a question in plain language. My-CoMind surfaces the full context behind any decision, instantly.
