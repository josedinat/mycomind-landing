How It Works

Three steps. The second and third happen on their own.

1

Capture

Hit record before a meeting, call or a walk-and-think — or just type it, or drop in a document. Live Capture keeps listening for as long as you need and can include the audio of the call itself, so the other side is captured too.

2

It organizes itself

My-CoMind pulls out the decisions, actions and open questions, then connects the memory to everything related in your history — including when a new decision supersedes or contradicts an older one. No tagging, no folders.

3

Come back and ask

Days or months later, open the workspace: where the topic stands, how it got here, what's unresolved. Ask a question in plain language and get an answer grounded in your own material — with the memories behind it.

Inside the Product

Built around continuity,
not note-taking

Four things that make the difference when you come back to a topic weeks later.

Live Capture: the whole meeting, not your memory of it

Start recording and keep talking. My-CoMind processes the conversation in short blocks, so you watch the decisions and open questions appear while the meeting is still happening.

  • Records for as long as you need — meetings, calls, classes, thinking out loud
  • Optionally captures the call's own audio, so the other participants are included
  • Decisions, action items and open questions extracted as you go
  • One recording becomes one memory — never a pile of fragments
  • Text and documents (PDF, DOCX) work the same way

A living workspace for every topic

Memories about the same thing gather into one workspace that stays current. Open it and you know where the topic stands in ten seconds — without re-reading twenty notes.

  • Where things stand, right now
  • Key milestones and how it got here
  • Decisions in effect — and which ones were superseded
  • Open points you can answer in place, which closes them
  • A senior specialist on that topic, ready to be asked

Cognitive Drift: how the thinking evolved

Most tools answer "what happened?". This one answers "how did the thinking change?" — the shift from one position to the next, with your own words on both sides and the time between them.

  • Each shift shown as before → after, with the real dates
  • How long a position actually held
  • Descriptive, never a judgement or a score
  • Ends with a question worth discussing, not a verdict
  • Available on every plan

One topic, compared across every project

Create an organization, give each initiative its own project, and share only what belongs there. Then search a single subject and see how each project handled it — side by side.

  • A project per initiative, client, or person you follow
  • Members see their own project; leadership sees across all of them
  • Memory Continuity: search one topic, get one reading per project
  • Project Synthesis: the standing read of where a project is
  • Shared memories stay owned by the person who shared them
